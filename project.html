SANRAL National Road Marking Project

Client SANRAL
Route Northern Cape to Western Cape
Contract Value R100 Million
Duration 6 Months
Service National Road Marking
Status Completed

Project Overview

Mthobothi (Pty) Ltd was appointed by the South African National Roads Agency (SANRAL) to carry out a national road marking project spanning the route from the Northern Cape to the Western Cape. The contract, valued at R100 million, was completed over a six-month construction period. The project was delivered fully within budget, in accordance with SANRAL's technical specifications, and within the agreed project duration.

Scope of Works

  • Road surface preparation along the Northern Cape to Western Cape route
  • Application of national road markings to SANRAL specification
  • Quality control and compliance inspection along the full route

Project Outcome

  • Completed within the R100 million contract budget
  • Completed within the six-month project duration
  • Delivered fully according to SANRAL specifications
