The Difference

RPC + BVLOS certified — most local operators aren't.

Most operators are limited to VLOS: short-range, visual-line-of-sight flying. Our BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) certification lets Maziya Drones legally cover large or remote sites — pipelines, farms, tower networks, transmission corridors — in fewer flights and faster turnaround.

That means larger contracts than competitors can legally bid on, and site coverage in hours rather than the weeks a ground crew would need.
