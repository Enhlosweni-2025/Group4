About Mthobothi

Civil Infrastructure & Earthworks

Mthobothi (Pty) Ltd is a Pietermaritzburg-based civil engineering contractor providing heavy civil infrastructure, structural earthworks, and concrete engineering services across the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands. Established in 2024, we work with municipal clients, developers, and industrial partners who need foundational work they can trust for generations.

We ensure precise foundational compliance for industrial developments, handling everything from bulk excavation to platform creation and advanced site levelling, backed by a plant hire fleet and CIDB-graded capacity suited to projects of scale.

Mission

To deliver civil infrastructure of lasting structural integrity, on time and to specification, for every client across the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands.

Vision

To be the region's most trusted name in civil earthworks and infrastructure, recognised for precision, safety, and durability.

  • Structural Integrity
  • Safety First
  • Precision Compliance
  • Community Investment
