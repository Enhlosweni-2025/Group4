On Every Job

Logged, insured, and checked before every take-off.

Every mission is recorded in an unmanned aircraft system pilot logbook, flown with insured, commercially registered equipment, and briefed against the current aeronautical chart for the operating area.

Flights are scheduled dynamically around weather windows, site access constraints and airspace restrictions — safety governs the calendar, not the other way around.

Common Questions

Compliance, answered directly.

01Are your drone operations legal and compliant?+
Yes. All flights are conducted by licensed Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) holders and comply with South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA) regulations.
02Do you carry insurance?+
Yes, we maintain public liability and equipment insurance for all commercial operations.
03What makes your certification different from other local operators?+
We hold RPC and BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) certification. Most local operators are limited to VLOS — short-range, visual-line-of-sight flying. BVLOS lets us legally cover large or remote sites such as pipelines, farms, tower networks and transmission corridors.
04What areas do you service?+
We're based in Pietermaritzburg and service clients across KwaZulu-Natal, with travel available for larger projects.
05How far in advance should I book?+
We recommend booking at least 3 days ahead, though we can accommodate shorter notice depending on availability and airspace clearance.
