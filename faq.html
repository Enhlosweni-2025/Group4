Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we're asked most often by municipal, industrial, and private clients. Can't find what you need? Use the contact form on our homepage.

General

What areas do you service?

Mthobothi (Pty) Ltd operates across Pietermaritzburg and the wider KwaZulu-Natal Midlands.

Do you handle municipal tenders?

Yes. We deliver to CIDB grading requirements and prepare documentation ready for state inspection.

Can you supply plant hire only?

Please contact us directly to discuss plant hire availability for your project.

How do I request a quote or tender submission?

Use the contact form below, call, or WhatsApp us directly and our team will respond promptly.

Do you service areas outside Pietermaritzburg?

Our core coverage is the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands, including surrounding towns. For projects further afield, contact us to confirm feasibility and mobilisation costs.

Do you provide free site assessments and quotes?

Yes. We conduct an initial site walk and issue a written quotation at no cost before any contract is agreed.

Project Scope & Capability

Can you handle both small and large-scale projects?

Yes. Our plant fleet and crew allocation scale from single-site earthworks to multi-hectare industrial platforms and municipal infrastructure contracts.

What types of ground and soil conditions can you work with?

We assess ground conditions during the site walk and adjust our approach — including compaction method and drainage design — to suit the soil type and terrain encountered.

Do you supply your own plant and machinery, or hire from suppliers?

We operate our own plant hire fleet, sized and graded for the project at hand, so equipment availability is managed directly rather than through third parties.

Do you handle emergency or urgent civil works?

Where capacity allows, we can prioritise urgent works such as road washaways or drainage failures. Contact us directly to discuss urgency and availability.

Can you provide compliance documentation for municipal inspections?

Yes. We maintain compaction test results, safety records, and site documentation throughout the project so it is ready for inspection at any stage, not only at completion.

Safety, Accreditation & Insurance

What safety standards do you follow?

All sites operate under daily inductions, mandatory PPE, and regular toolbox talks, supported by our zero-incident track record to date.

Are you insured and CIDB accredited?

Yes, our CIDB grading and insurance details are provided as part of our tender and quotation documentation on request.

What environmental precautions do you take on site?

We manage stormwater and erosion control during earthworks, minimise unnecessary vegetation clearing, and follow site-specific environmental conditions where applicable to the project.

Timelines, Payment & Warranty

What is your typical project turnaround time?

Timelines depend on project scope and ground conditions. A programme with milestone dates is issued alongside your quotation once scope is confirmed.

Do you offer warranties on completed work?

Yes, structural and earthworks components are covered by workmanship terms agreed in the project contract, which we're happy to walk through before work begins.

How do payment terms and milestones work?

Payment terms are agreed per contract, typically structured against milestones such as mobilisation, progress, and completion. Full terms are set out in the project agreement.

Community & References

Do you employ local labour from the community?

Yes. Community investment is one of our core values, and we prioritise local labour and local subcontractors on our projects wherever possible.

Can I request references from previous clients?

Yes, references and further project examples can be requested directly — contact us and we'll share relevant contacts for your type of project.

Still Have a Question?

Reach out directly and our team will get back to you.
