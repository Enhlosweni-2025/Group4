Who We Are

A commercial drone operator built around one idea: better decisions from the sky down.

Maziya Drones (PTY) LTD is a registered, RPC- and BVLOS-certified drone operator, operating under ABEOD's UASOC, offering aerial surveying, mapping and inspection services across South Africa's infrastructure, telecom, construction and agricultural sectors.

Traditional ground-based surveying is slow — often weeks per project — costly, and risky, from manual tower climbs to prolonged site access. Maziya Drones delivers site coverage in hours and reports in days, at 50–75% lower cost and with higher, RTK-grade accuracy, without the need for ground control points.

Founded and led by Phumulani Eugen Mashele, every project receives hands-on management from initial booking and flight planning through to data processing and final delivery.
