Who We Are

Building the Midlands' Infrastructure, One Foundation at a Time

Mthobothi (Pty) Ltd is a Pietermaritzburg-based civil engineering contractor providing heavy civil infrastructure, structural earthworks, and concrete engineering services across the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands. Established in 2024, we work with municipal clients, private developers, and industrial partners who need foundational work they can trust for generations.

Our team combines hands-on site experience with a disciplined, documentation-first approach to project delivery. From the first site walk to final sign-off, every stage of our work is planned against grading specifications, safety protocol, and the compliance standards our municipal and industrial clients require.

We ensure precise foundational compliance for industrial developments, handling everything from bulk excavation to platform creation and advanced site levelling, backed by a plant hire fleet and CIDB-graded capacity suited to projects of scale. Whether the brief is a single access road or a full industrial platform, we scope, resource, and deliver it with the same rigour.

Mission

To deliver civil infrastructure of lasting structural integrity, on time and to specification, for every client across the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands.

Vision

To be the region's most trusted name in civil earthworks and infrastructure, recognised for precision, safety, and durability.

Our Approach

Every project begins with a detailed site assessment and geotechnical review, so that grading, drainage, and load-bearing decisions are made with full knowledge of ground conditions. We plan plant and crew allocation around the project timeline agreed with the client, and we report progress against milestones rather than waiting until completion to surface issues.

We also invest directly in the communities where we work — prioritising local labour, local subcontractors, and skills transfer on every site we operate, in line with our community investment value.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These principles guide every decision on site, from the first excavation to final handover.

Structural Integrity

We engineer every foundation, platform, and pour to specification — not to the minimum, but to what will genuinely last under load and weather over decades.

Safety First

Our safety record reflects a culture, not a checklist: toolbox talks, PPE compliance, and site inductions are non-negotiable on every project, every day.

Precision Compliance

Municipal and industrial clients need documentation as much as they need concrete. We keep inspection-ready records throughout, not just at the end.

Community Investment

We prioritise local labour and local subcontractors on our projects, so the economic benefit of the work stays in the communities we build in.
